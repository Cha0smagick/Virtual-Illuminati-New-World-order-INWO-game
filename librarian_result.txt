## ⚠️ THREE CRITICAL CORRECTIONS TO YOUR ASSUMPTIONS

Before the rules: three of your stated assumptions are **wrong for INWO** (they come from the *original* 1982 Illuminati board game, not INWO). Verified against the v1.2 rulebook text:

1. **There is NO money in INWO.** The word "money" appears exactly once in the 20-page rulebook (in the *Criminal* alignment description); "income" appears **zero** times. The economy is **Action Tokens**, not dollars. There is no starting money, no income phase, no money-to-power conversion.
2. **Groups do NOT print income.** Group cards print **Power** and **Resistance** only. (The original Illuminati game had income; INWO removed it.)
3. **There is NO "Neutralize" attack.** The word "neutralize" appears **zero** times in the rulebook. INWO has three attack types: **Attack to Control**, **Attack to Destroy**, and **Instant Attacks** (Assassinations/Disasters). The closest concept to "neutralize" is the **Cancel** mechanic (p. 15) and **Paralyze** (Plot family).

# SECTION A: RULES DIGEST (v1.2, verbatim where critical)

## A.1 Setup / Beginning the Game

- **Deck**: each player builds a 45-card deck (including Illuminati). "typically, a strong deck will have 12-20 Group cards and 24-32 Plot cards." Cards split into a Plot deck (Plots + extra Illuminati) and Group deck (Groups + Resources).
- **Starting hand**: **3 Plot cards** (step 4) + **6 Group cards** (step 6). Plus one chosen "lead" Group placed on an Illuminati control arrow (step 5). "You may not lead with a Resource — it must be a Group!"
- **First player**: "Each player rolls two dice. The one with the highest roll goes first."
- **First-turn protection**: "At the beginning of the game, you may not do anything to a rival who has not yet completed his first turn!" (Exception: if someone attacks you on his first turn, you may respond.)
- **No money, no income** — confirmed above.

## A.2 Turn Sequence (7 steps, v1.2)

> **Beginning of Turn** — during this portion you may only use Action tokens to buy Plot cards, power Plots/abilities that affect these phases, or respond.
> 1. **Draw** the top card from your Plot deck, if you wish. (May exchange Action tokens for extra Plot draws.)
> 2. **Draw** the top card from your Group deck, if you wish.
> 3. **One automatic takeover**, if you wish — play any Group or Resource from hand with **no die roll**. "You may not duplicate a Group already in play unless a card specifically allows it."
> 4. **Place an Action token on each of your Groups that doesn't already have one.** Resources with the word "Action" also get tokens.
> **Main Phase**
> 5. Attacks and other Main Phase actions, any number, any order.
> **End of Turn**
> 6. Use Plots/abilities that happen "at the end of your turn."
> 7. **Knock.** "At this time, any player(s) (including the one who just knocked) who has achieved one of his Goals may declare victory." (v1.2 moved victory declaration to *after* knocking.)

## A.3 Action Tokens (the economy)

- **Each Group that has no Action token gets one at the beginning of your turn.** Tokens **persist** if unused (you may spend leftover tokens for Plot draws just before getting new ones).
- **Illuminati gets 1 Action token** (it is a Group; UFOs get **2** — their special ability; FAQ: "If I'm playing the UFOs, place my two Action tokens… can I keep the extra token?").
- A Group **cannot get tokens if its Power is reduced to 0** (printed-Power-0 groups get tokens normally).
- A Group spends its token to attack, aid, oppose, or use certain abilities. "Thus, a Group can only do one of these things each turn, unless it somehow gets an extra Action token."
- **Token → Plot conversion**: "you may exchange **one Action token from your Illuminati or two Action tokens from your other Groups** for one Plot card drawn from your deck." This is a **Free Move** (cannot be canceled).
- Resource tokens **cannot** be traded for Plot cards.
- **Illuminati action** (Main Phase, once per turn each): play a Resource from hand, or draw an extra Group card.

## A.4 Power Structure & Control Arrows

- **Illuminati cards have four outgoing control arrows.** "Each of these can be used to control one Group."
- **Other Groups have one incoming control arrow, and 0 to 3 outgoing control arrows.** (There is **no global "8 groups" cap** — the limit is the arrow count. The number 8 appears only in the Plot hand limit and Basic Goal.)
- Placement: "put its incoming arrow next to an outgoing arrow of its master… It does not matter if a card is upside-down or sideways, as long as the arrows line up properly and no Group overlaps any other Group."
- **Tree structure**: master → puppets; puppets may have their own puppets. "Groups in your Power Structure may never overlap."
- **Adjacency for defense**: "A Group which is directly controlled by the Illuminati gets a **+10** to Resistance! If it is one group away, it gets a **+5**. If it's farther away, it gets no bonus."
- **Moving Groups**: Main Phase, cost 1 Action token (from the Group, its old/new master, or your Illuminati). "You may move any Group you control to any open control arrow on any Group in play." Puppets move with it.
- **Gifts/Trades**: hand cards may be traded/given at any time (except during Privileged attacks); Power-Structure groups may be given away during Main Phase (costs an action); Resources may be given away if unused that turn.

## A.5 Attack to Control (the core formula)

> "The **strength** of the attack is the **attacker's Power minus the defender's Resistance**. You must roll that number or less, on two dice. So roll low! **You must get your attack's strength to at least 2, because that is the lowest number you can roll on two dice.** An attack with a lower strength cannot succeed."

- **Cost**: attacking Group spends an Action token **and must have at least one open control arrow**.
- **Automatic Failure**: "A roll of 11 or 12 always means the attack failed, no matter how much Power was involved."
- **Alignment modifiers to attack strength**: "If the attacking and defending Groups have any identical alignments, the attack strength gets a **+4 bonus for each one**. If they have any opposite alignments, the attack strength gets a **–4 penalty for each one**. (Remember, any two Fanatic Groups are opposite to each other!)"
- **Resistance modifiers (defense)**:
  - **Controlling Alignment**: "+4 to its Resistance for every alignment that is the same as that of its master (except Fanatic)."
  - **Power Structure Position**: +10 (directly under Illuminati) / +5 (one away).
  - **Special abilities** as printed.
- **Aiding/opposing**: an aiding/opposing Group spends an Action token and adds its Power. "A Group can aid an Attack to Control if it has at least one alignment identical to the target. A Group can oppose… if it has at least one alignment identical to the target, or if it is the target's master or puppet." Aiding/opposing Groups **do not** get alignment bonuses/penalties.
- **Global Power**: Groups with a second Power number (e.g., 6/2) may use the second number to aid/oppose **regardless of alignments**. "A Group can never have Global Power higher than its regular Power."
- **Self-defense doubling (v1.2)**: "The Power of an action spent by a Group to defend itself is **doubled**. If the action's Power is already doubled, it is **tripled** instead; if it's already tripled, it is **quadrupled** instead; and so on! If the target has more than one token, it can spend them all, and each one gets the bonus!"
- **Calculation order (Meta-Rule)**: "changes to a specific value come first, then multiplication or division, then addition or subtraction. Multipliers are not cumulative; use only the single largest multiplier."
- **Whoops!**: "If you forget to declare a bonus during an attack, whether it's for attack or defense, you lose it. When the dice are rolled, it's over."
- **Results — success**: target captured; place under the attacking Group; its puppets are captured too (keep relative positions; overlapping new cards may be rearranged; unfittable ones discarded). "A newly-captured Group does not get an Action token."
- **Results — failure**: "If the target belonged to another player, it stays where it was. If it came from your own hand… if you have not succeeded by the end of your turn, your agents are revealed and eliminated — **you must discard that Group card!**"

## A.6 Attack to Destroy

Same as Control except:
1. Roll **"Power minus Power"** (target defends with Power, not Resistance). Closeness bonus still counts (unless destroying your own Group); master-alignment bonus does **not**.
2. "Unlike Groups destroy each other more easily. The strength of an Attack to Destroy gets a **+4 bonus for each opposite alignment, and a –4 penalty for each identical alignment.**"
3. To **aid** a Destroy attack, a Group needs an alignment **opposite** to the target; to oppose, same rules as Control.
4. **No open control arrow needed.**
5. Success → target goes to your **destroyed pile** (track who destroyed what — Goals depend on it).
6. Target's puppets are **not** destroyed — they lose tokens and return to the owner's hand.
7. You may destroy your own Groups (but not from your hand; no Group may attack itself).

## A.7 Instant Attacks, Assassinations, Disasters

- **Instant Attack** = Attack to Destroy launched by a Plot card (e.g., Assassination, Disaster). "The strength of an Instant Attack is the Power of the attack minus the Power of the target Group at the moment the attack is declared." Target gets its closeness bonus. **No other modifiers apply unless the card says so.** Groups cannot spend actions to make/aid/oppose Instants unless a card permits. Target may not spend tokens to defend. May be played at any time (even during a rival's turn, except during another attack).
- **Assassination** = Instant Attack to Destroy a **Personality**. "A Personality destroyed by an Assassination is **killed**, and may only be saved or returned to play by cards that specifically say that they restore killed or assassinated Personalities."
- **Disaster** = attack (usually Instant) to destroy a **Place**. "The target of a Disaster always loses one Action token, if it had any, as soon as the Disaster card is played."
- **Devastation & Relief**: Devastated Place loses its tokens and those of all groups below it; those groups "cannot get Action tokens and do not count toward victory." Devastated Place's Power is **halved (round down)** vs. Destroy attacks. **Relief**: "spend actions with a total Power **three times the printed Power** of the Devastated Place" (any players, all at once).

## A.8 Privilege & Immunity

- **Privileged Attack**: attacker declares "Privilege!" when announcing. "No player other than the attacker and the defender can interfere." Third parties cannot aid/oppose/cancel/give cards. Anyone may play a card that **negates** the Privilege (then it can't be regained).
- **Immunity**: "If Group A is immune to Group B, then Group B cannot attack Group A, aid attacks on Group A, or affect Group A with its special abilities. If your entire Power Structure is immune to a certain Group, then all your Groups are immune, and that Group cannot use any special ability to affect your Resources, hand, decks, or discards." Immunity **does not** protect against Plot cards. "You can never be immune to something you yourself control."

## A.9 Secret Groups

- Secret Groups "can **only** be affected by Illuminati groups, by other Secret groups, and by groups whose special abilities let them aid or attack Secret groups." Master/puppets may defend and aid (if otherwise eligible). Resources affect Secret groups normally unless linked to a non-Secret Group. **Plots** may affect Secret groups (even powered by non-Secret actions).

## A.10 Drawing, Hand Limit, Discarding

- **Draws**: 1 Plot + 1 Group at start of turn (optional); extra Plot per 1 Illuminati token or 2 Group tokens; extra Group per Illuminati action (once/turn). "You are never required to draw cards, and there is no penalty for running out of cards in your deck."
- **Plot hand limit**: "When it is not your turn, you may hold up to **5 Plot cards** in your hand. Both hidden and exposed Plots count against your limit." During your own turn there is **no limit**. Group/Resource cards: no limit ever.
- **Returning Plots**: you may return a Plot to your deck (top/bottom/middle) at any time — **not** Group/Resource cards.
- **Discards**: face-up; "You may discard any card in your hand at any time, except in the middle of a multiple-card draw or immediately after someone attempts to look at or steal your cards." Discarded = permanently removed unless immediately recovered.

## A.11 Resources

- Purple cards, drawn from the Group deck. "Normally, they can be played only as an automatic takeover. You may also spend a **single** Illuminati action each turn to take over a Resource."
- "Resources have no alignments or Control Arrows, and don't go into your Power Structure. They are placed beside it."
- All Resources belong to your Illuminati **unless linked** to another Group. If a Group is captured, its linked Resources go with it; if destroyed, its linked Resources are destroyed.
- **Types**: Artifact, Gadget, Magic, Unique. "A Unique Resource is a one-of-a-kind item. Once a Unique Resource is brought into play, no further copies… may be played."
- Some Resources have "Action" at the bottom → they get Action tokens (not tradable for Plots).
- **No normal way to attack a Resource** (some Plots/abilities can).

## A.12 Special Plot families

- **+10 Plots**: give +10 Power or Resistance for one action or defense; "does not count for any Goal"; may only count once per action/defense.
- **Attribute Freeze**: prevents all Groups with a certain attribute from acting (except self-defense) for the turn, or cancels one action.
- **Paralyze**: "A paralyzed Group cannot spend Action tokens and cannot use any special ability or linked Resource… Control of a paralyzed Group does not count for any Goal."
- **Power Increase**: linked to a Group of a certain type, sets Power to the stated value.
- **Zap**: affects an entire Power Structure until removed; any player may spend an Illuminati action to remove all Zaps from one player.
- **NWO cards**: played to the center, affect all players; three colors (red/blue/yellow), only one of each color in play; replacing a same-color NWO discards the old one. "There can never be more than three NWO cards in effect at once!"
- **Goal cards**: a type of Plot; "No player may have more than one Goal card in his hand." Revealed (not played) during a victory attempt; cannot be stolen/canceled during the attempt; if the attempt fails, the Goal is returned to hand **exposed**. "If a player's Plots are exposed at any time and he has too many Goal cards, he's out of the game."
- **Hidden Agents**: a duplicate Group card in hand may be played when that Group is attacked: **+10 to aid, –6 to oppose**. One agents card per attack.

## A.13 Victory Conditions

- **Elimination**: "A player is eliminated if, at any time after his **third complete turn**, his Illuminati has no puppets."
- **Win by destroying all rivals.**
- **No one can win during the first round** — earliest victory is end of the first player's second turn.
- **Basic Goal** (v1.2 standard): "**12 Groups for a game with two or three players, 11 Groups for a game with four players, or 10 Groups for a game with five or more players.**" (Adjustable by mutual consent; WDH 1.1 recommended 10 with starter decks, 12 for two-player.)
- **Special Goal**: printed on each Illuminati card (see B.4).
- **Goal Cards**: alternate victory conditions (see B.5).
- **Counting double**: "No Group can ever count more than double… and no player may count more than **three** Groups double."
- **Shared victory**: "If two or more players both meet their Goals at the same time, they share the victory." Exception: same-Illuminati factions cannot share (except Shangri-La).
- **Permanent vs. temporary changes**: permanent Power/alignment changes count for Goals; temporary Power bonuses do **not**; temporary alignment changes **do** count (only for a victory declared that turn).

## A.14 Two-Player Rules (official for tournaments)

- "Never set the Basic Goal to less than 12 Groups."
- "Neither player may attack the other until each has taken a full turn."
- "A player who takes an automatic takeover during Phase 3 of his turn loses one of the Illuminati action tokens he would normally get during Phase 4 of that turn."

## A.15 The "Uncontrolled Area" (One Big Deck / OWE variant)

From the official **One Big Deck** variant (the OWE-specific rules):
- "When any player tries to control a card from his hand and fails, that card goes into the uncontrolled, or neutral, area. (Exception: The special power of the **Adepts of Hermes** means they keep a group even if they fail their attempt to control it.) And if a player drops out or is destroyed, the groups in his hand go into the neutral area."
- "Any player may try to take over or to destroy a card from the uncontrolled area… However, you may not make an automatic takeover from the uncontrolled area."
- OBD starting hand: **3 Plots + 10 Groups** (variant); each player chooses an Illuminati from the Plot deck.
- OBD worthless-card list (discard & redraw): Media Blitz, Counter-Revolution, Payoff, Purge, Faction Fight, Clone, Impostor, Forgery, etc. Remove Voodoo Economics and March on Washington.

## Coverage gaps (honest assessment)

1. **Full Power/Resistance/Alignment stats for all 412 cards are NOT freely available online.** The official SJG card list gives names/types/rarity only. The only complete public source of every card's stats and text is **The INWO Book** (SJG, $10 PDF, https://www.sjgames.com/inwo/INWObook/) — "Every Card Described: All 412 cards from the Limited Edition, with the complete text of every card." **I recommend buying it**; it is the definitive source for your JS database and will let you fill every gap.
2. **Verified stats above cover ~40 cards** (from the rulebook's worked examples, official FAQ/errata, and the CMU game log). The remaining ~370 cards' stats need The INWO Book or the physical cards.
3. **Illuminati Power values** for Adepts of Hermes, Bermuda Triangle, Discordian Society, Gnomes of Zurich, Shangri-La, and UFOs are marked unverified (commonly 8 in the base set, but confirm against the cards).
4. The OWE set = 403 base cards + 3× each Illuminati + blanks; the official list's 412 count includes Limited/Unlimited variants of a few cards (Air Magic, Good Polls, L-4 Society, Israel, Texas, etc.) — in OWE these are the Limited-print versions with black backs.
