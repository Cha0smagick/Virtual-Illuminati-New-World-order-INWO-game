Public History of an INWO Game

This is a history of the beginning of an online game of Illuminati: New World Order. This history has links that show every card that players had in their hand, and every conspiracy that they remembered to show me. Those links, however, will be private only to me until the game is over.

The players, with links to their decks:

  • Don Fnordlioni (donfnord@io.com)
  • James Eddleman (James.Eddleman@Sciatl.com)
  • Thany (sherlock@ksu.edu)
  • Jason Bostick (macro@proaxis.com)
  • Glen Barnett (g.barnett@unsw.edu.au)

Ralph Melton (ralph@cs.cmu.edu) served as moderator and coordinator for the game. The word I in this document refers to Ralph.

The links for the names of cards look up the cards in Wendell Hicken's Card List Generator.

The game was played by Pittsburgh House Rules, with the additional rule that overlapping cards did not matter, because the position of control arrows was inconvenient for us to look up and to represent textually.

We decided before the game began that we would play to 12 groups for the Basic Goal.
